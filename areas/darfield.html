Built for Darfield winters

Inland Canterbury gets proper frosts, and Darfield winter mornings are a lot colder than in the city. A correctly sized heat pump is one of the cheapest ways to keep the house warm, and good ventilation stops the damp and condensation that come with the cold. We size both for how cold it actually gets here, not for a Christchurch suburb.

Older homes and wiring

If your home still has an old fuse board with ceramic fuses, or the wiring hasn't been looked at in decades, it's worth getting it checked. A switchboard upgrade with RCD protection is one of the best safety improvements you can make to an older house.

What is an RCD and do I need one?

An RCD (residual current device) is a safety switch that cuts the power in a split second if it detects current leaking to earth, for example through a person. Many older homes don't have them. Adding RCD protection, usually as part of a switchboard upgrade, is one of the best safety improvements you can make.

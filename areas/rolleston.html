Cold bedrooms in a newer home?

Plenty of newer homes were built with one heat pump in the living area and nothing down the bedroom end. If the back of the house is cold in winter, a second smaller unit is often the fix. We'll look at the layout and tell you honestly what will make a difference.

Rolleston electrician FAQs

Do you cover Rolleston?

Yes. Rolleston is about half an hour from our Darfield base, and we work there regularly.

My house has one heat pump but the bedrooms are still cold. What can I do?

A second, smaller heat pump at the bedroom end of the house is often the simplest fix. In some homes better ventilation helps too, because dry air is easier to heat. We'll look at the layout and recommend the option that makes the most difference for the money.

Do you work for small businesses in Rolleston?

Yes. As well as homes, we do electrical work for small businesses, shops and offices, including lighting, power, repairs and switchboard work.

Sheffield electrician FAQs

Do you cover Sheffield and Springfield?

Yes. Sheffield is about 10 minutes from our Darfield base, and we also cover Waddington, Springfield and the surrounding area.

Will a heat pump work in Sheffield's cold winters?

Yes, if it's the right one. Modern heat pumps keep heating well below freezing outside, but they need to be chosen and sized for it. We'll pick a unit that suits foothill temperatures.

My power keeps tripping. Is that dangerous?

A tripping switch is a safety device doing its job, but it means something is wrong, such as a faulty appliance, damaged wiring or water getting in somewhere. Don't keep resetting it. Give us a call and we'll track down the cause.

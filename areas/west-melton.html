Can you do the electrical for my new build in West Melton?

Do I need ventilation as well as a heat pump?

In many newer homes, yes. Modern houses are well sealed, so moisture from cooking, showers and drying clothes has nowhere to go. A ventilation system keeps the air dry and fresh, and a dry home is easier and cheaper to heat.

Common questions

Is it worth switching to LED lights?

Usually, yes. LEDs use far less power than old halogen or incandescent bulbs and last many years longer, so you save on your power bill and on replacement bulbs.

Can you replace old halogen downlights?

Yes. Old halogen downlights run hot and use a lot of power. We can swap them for modern LED downlights, which run cooler and are safer around ceiling insulation.

Do you do outdoor and security lighting?

Yes. We install outdoor lighting for decks, gardens, driveways and paths, as well as sensor and security lights.

Can you add more lights or dimmers to an existing room?

Yes. We can add new lights, move switches, fit dimmers and add power points, and we'll tidy up after ourselves.

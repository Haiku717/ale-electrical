Electrical repairs & fault finding

Something not working right? We track down the problem and fix it safely, so your home is back to normal fast.

Electrical faults are frustrating and, if they're left alone, they can be dangerous too. We have the gear and the know-how to find the root cause, not just patch over it. We'll explain what went wrong in plain English and sort it properly.

What we can fix

  • Tripping breakers and blown fuses
  • Dead or sparking power points
  • Faulty switches and dimmers
  • Flickering or failing lights
  • Hot water and appliance circuits
  • General safety checks and testing

Safe, honest and tidy

If something's not safe, we'll tell you straight and won't do work you don't need. You'll get a clear price before we start, and the job left clean and tidy when we're done.

Worried it might be urgent?

If you can smell burning, see sparks or have lost power to part of the house, switch it off at the board and give us a call. We'll talk you through what to do and get to you as soon as we can.
